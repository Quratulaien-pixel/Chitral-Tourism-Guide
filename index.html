Festivals & Events

Experience the colorful traditions, sports, music, and cultural celebrations of Chitral.

🌸

Chilam Joshi Festival

📅 May 13–16

A vibrant annual spring celebration of the Kalash people, featuring colorful traditional attire, music, dancing, cultural rituals, and sacred milk offerings.

🏇

Shandur Polo Festival

📅 July

An iconic annual sports and cultural event featuring thrilling freestyle polo between Chitral and Gilgit-Baltistan.

🌾

Uchal Festival

📅 August

An ancient Kalash harvest celebration featuring traditional dancing, folk music, prayers, food, and community gatherings.

❄️

Chaumos Festival

📅 December

The major winter and New Year celebration of the Kalash people, featuring purification rituals, bonfires, traditional singing, and dancing.
